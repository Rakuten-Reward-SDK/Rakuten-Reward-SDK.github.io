What You Get

One SDK. Retention and monetization, built in.

Rakuten Reward Native SDK covers everything — mission-based point rewards and premium ad delivery — in a single integration.

Mission-Based Retention

Define missions tied to any in-app action — daily launches, level clears, registrations — and reward users with Rakuten Points on completion to boost DAU and session frequency.

Ad Monetization

Serve Rakuten's premium ad inventory — including Rakuten Ichiba product ads — auto-optimized by member ID, generating ad revenue for any app type.

100M+ Member Reach

Rakuten's media network actively directs its member base toward partner apps, giving you an acquisition channel beyond the traditional app stores.

Built-in Portal UI

A ready-to-use mission list and notification portal you can launch with a single API call — no custom UI design required.

Flexible KPI Targeting

Design mission campaigns around any KPI — acquisition, activation, engagement, or re-engagement — and iterate without shipping an app update.

Cross-Platform

Native SDKs for Android and iOS, plus a JavaScript SDK — works for ad-supported, paid, and non-monetized apps alike.

How It Works

From zero to live in about two weeks

SDK integration itself takes roughly one person-day. The rest is campaign setup and review — then you're earning with your users.

1

Install SDK

Add via Maven, CocoaPods, SPM, or npm depending on your platform.

2

Initialize

Configure with your App Key and App Secret at app launch.

3

Define Missions

Create missions in the Reward dashboard and map them to in-app actions.

4

Log Actions

Call the action API when users complete tasks — points are awarded automatically.
